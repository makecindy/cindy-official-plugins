## Notes for contributors

- When adding a plugin, **prefer an original or generic icon** over a third
  party's official logo. `cindy-art`, `cindy-web-search`, `cindy-mermaid`,
  `icloud-mail`, and `google-calendar` already use generic artwork.
- If a third-party logo is genuinely needed, check that brand's own usage
  guidelines first (some explicitly forbid third parties from using their logo as
  a product icon), and add a row to the table above.
- Do not recolour, restretch, or reshape a third-party logo before using it —
  most brand guidelines prohibit this, and it carries more risk than using the
  mark as-is. The existing `google-gmail` icon is a recoloured Gmail mark and is
  flagged above for review; do not treat it as a precedent.
