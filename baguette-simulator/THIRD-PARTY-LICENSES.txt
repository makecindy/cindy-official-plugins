Official Baguette logo sourced from https://github.com/tddworks/baguette/blob/v0.1.98/assets/logo.png

0.1.2: Official logo resized to 512 × 512 PNG for Cindy’s 512 KiB icon limit; artwork unchanged.

0.1.3 modification: bundled Web/stream-session.js reconnects after an unexpected WebSocket close, releases old decoder/timers, and cancels reconnect on deliberate stop. Native Baguette binary remains unchanged.

0.1.4 modification: bundled Web/baguette/parts/keyboard.js routes user clipboard actions through a loopback bridge scoped to the private device set, with exact Origin and random capability checks and visible failure feedback. Native binary unchanged.

0.1.6 itemized modifications: native/keyboard.m adapts the CoreSimulator resolution and SimulatorKit HID protocol from Baguette v0.1.98 (Apache-2.0), adds completion acknowledgements, exclusive device lock and release-only recovery; compiled as native/keyboard (Apple Silicon, ad-hoc signed). Web/baguette/parts/keyboard.js routes all keyboard/text input through the acknowledged adapter, suppresses repeats and serializes key bursts. Web/baguette/parts/bezel.js, Web/baguette/carplay/carplay-frame.js and Web/sim-native.html replace crosshair with default cursor. Original Baguette binary remains unchanged.
